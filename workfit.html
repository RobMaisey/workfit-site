The problem

You doubled your wellness budget. Nothing moved.

Not because the budget was wrong. Because the frame was.

Four frames control how organisations think about mental health at work. None of them starts from the actual problem.

Human Resources frames it as risk.

HR exists to ensure compliance and regulation, so the outputs look like risk management.

The medical field frames it as disorder.

Diagnosis is the barrier to access, so people pursue a label to get help. The outputs look like illness management.

Health and safety frames it as a hazard to eliminate.

The same logic that applies to a chainsaw gets applied to human psychology, with a claims process behind it. The outputs look like hazard control and injury management.

The organisation frames it as readily accessible.

If you need help, it is there, just ask. But struggling people do not identify as having a work problem. They cope. They may not even be aware. Their solution becomes your problem: more absence, more conflict, more management time, less output.

I move people up the scale. The old model waits to catch them as they fall.

The real problem

You have unhappy employees and you want happy ones. How happy your people are is the most direct mental health indicator you have. You cannot make someone happy. But regular maintenance across every part of their life means personal problems do not become operational problems.

WorkFit is an operational solution to an operational problem.

The approach

Preventative maintenance for your people

WorkFit is a partnership-based program. It puts proactive, confidential support inside your organisation before problems escalate.

A visible, approachable presence your people can reach.

2

Proactive interventions

Scheduled check-ins that catch small issues early.

3

Personalised resilience tools

Practical skills tailored to each person's situation.

4

Confidential coaching

Private, judgement-free conversations, in person or remote.

How it works: a partnership model

  • On-site presence: educational sessions and visible, ongoing support.
  • Proactive check-ins: scheduled outreach for early identification.
  • Confidential conversations: private discussions, in person or remote.
  • Wellness program integration: works alongside your existing initiatives.
  • Optional on-call phone support: accessible guidance, with manager approval.

Flexible, scalable, and cost-effective. Voluntary for employees, and you can cancel at any time.
