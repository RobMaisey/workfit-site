The idea

Most conflict is a gap in how two people see the same thing

Mental health is largely the distance between how you see the world and how the people around you see it.

Two people sit through the same event and walk away with different stories about what happened. The event did not change. The seeing did. That gap is where the friction lives, at home, at work, with the people closest to you.

Work on the seeing and the conflict has somewhere to go. Leave it alone and the same argument keeps arriving in new clothes.

If you are reaching for marriage counselling

Most people need individual work first

Couples counselling treats the relationship as the unit. The friction usually starts one layer down, in how each person sees themselves and reads the other. Sort out how you relate to yourself and most of what shows up as a relationship problem changes shape.

This is coaching, not therapy. We work forward from where you are, on how you see, decide, and act. If what you need is clinical care, I will tell you, and point you to the right person.
