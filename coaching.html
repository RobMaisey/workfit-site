The idea

Old programs keep running

Most of what gets in the way is an old program: a way of reacting that made sense once and keeps running after it has stopped working for you.

It shows up between you and other people, whether that is a team that does not deliver what you expected, a partner you keep having the same argument with, or a manager you cannot read.

The other person is rarely the thing to fix. The program is: how you read the situation, what you tell yourself about it, and what that makes you do.

The method

Self-awareness first, then behaviour change

We start by noticing the program: the thought, the feeling it sets off, and what you do next. Once you can see it running, you can choose a different response at the point where it goes wrong, and practise it until the new one holds.

I show you where to look and how to practise. The change itself is yours to make.

If you are reaching for couples counselling, individual work usually comes first. The friction tends to start in how each person reads the other, and that is work you can do on your own.

This is coaching, not therapy. We work forward from where you are. If what you need is clinical care, I will tell you, and point you to the right person.
