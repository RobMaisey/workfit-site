The idea

Past you and future you

How often do the thoughts and emotions of the person you have become get in the way of the person you want to be?

Past you is a rule book built from old experience: beliefs, judgements, memories, and the stories your brain keeps writing to match them. Future you is the person you want to be, built on choice and purpose. The friction lives between the two.

Nothing happens in the past or the future. Action only happens in the present. Past you pulls you back into guilt, regret and replaying what went wrong. Future you, left unmanaged, turns into worry and "what if". The work brings both into the present, where a decision can be made.

What we work on

Four parts of the same work

Core values

Your own, not someone else's definition of success. They become the test for every decision you make.

Self-awareness

Catching the old rule as it fires, hearing the self-talk, and spotting the always-and-never thinking that narrows your options.

Emotional awareness

Naming the emotion and pinning it to the actual thing it is about, so you choose a response instead of running the reaction.

Relationships

Saying what you need, holding people to clear expectations without blame, and owning your part.

The method

Awareness, choice, action, reset

Your habits will always overpower your choices until you make changing them the choice.

Mental skills are trained, not innate. We work through the same process until the new response holds: notice what is running, choose what you want to do instead, do it, and reset when it slips. I show you where to look and how to practise. The change itself is yours to make.

This is coaching, not therapy. We work forward from where you are. If what you need is clinical care, I will tell you, and point you to the right person.
