WorkFit Tools

Practical, free mental-health tools. No login required. Use them yourself or share the link with anyone.

People don't experience the same situation the same way. Different operating systems. Different assumptions about who owns the problem. These short, interactive tools make those differences visible so people can move forward.

Time Travel

Maps which brain system is running, the thinking patterns behind it, and a path forward.

Drama Triangle

Shows how unaddressed problems pull people into roles and conflict, then offers a way out.

Perceptual Positions

Explores any situation from three points of view, including the outside observer, to build understanding.
