About Rob & the WorkFit approach

A journey to proactive support.

My story

From adversity to advocacy

My approach to coaching isn't from a textbook. It's built on a lifetime of overcoming challenges and helping others do the same.

I've spent 20+ years across different worlds: commodity lumber trading, entrepreneurship, raising kids at home, and coaching sport. Mentorship and hard-won experience taught me how people change, and how much steadier life gets with the right tools.

My philosophy

Understanding & empowerment

I combine behavioural-change methods with practical mental-health support. People reframe how they see their situation and build resilience, then keep the tools long after our work together.

Why I do this

So people and teams can thrive

My mission is to build environments where people and the teams around them do more than cope. Stronger individuals make more cohesive, capable teams.
